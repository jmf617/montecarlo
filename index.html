Guyton-Klinger Guardrails ▼
Preservation Trigger (% above initial WR)
+20%
How far your current WR must rise above your starting WR before the preservation cut fires. At 20%: if you started at 6.5% WR, the cut fires when WR hits 7.8%. Higher = more tolerance before cutting — less safe
Prosperity Trigger (% below initial WR)
-20%
How far your WR must fall below starting WR before you get a spending increase. At 20%: increase fires when WR drops to 5.2%. Portfolio is clearly outperforming. Higher = harder to unlock bonus spending
Preservation Cut %
10% spend cut
How much spending is cut when the preservation trigger fires. 20% cut on $130k = drop to $104k. Larger cut = more portfolio protection in bad years. Higher = deeper cut = safer portfolio
Core Floor Basis
Real — core floor rises with inflation
ON (correct): the protected core floor is stated in today's dollars and rises with inflation, so a $75k floor still buys $75k of goods in year 40. OFF: the floor is a fixed nominal number that never rises, so over a long horizon the guardrail can cut real spending by 70%+ and still report the plan as a success. Turning this OFF will materially raise reported success rates. It does not make the plan safer. Off = flattering, not safer
Prosperity Increase %
10% flex increase
How much spending increases when the prosperity trigger fires. Reward for portfolio outperformance. Does not affect failure scenarios. Higher = more upside in good years
